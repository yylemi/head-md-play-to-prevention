# games within a game
a dream within a dream. games within a game. 
## pitch
Interface of a game within another board game. You can't play whichever game you want whenever you want, but you get these games randomly after throwing a dice to advance. The objective is to finish the mini-games that you end up playing successfully.
## lists
- having a fake game interface where you can switch  different games because you HAVE TO, kind of like mario party
- you have to throw the dice and advance through a game board

# visual novel game 
The most simplest idea that came to my mind,.
## pitch
A visual novel where the main interaction is different choices, you play as a manipulating bully try to navigate social dynamics at your new school. You got expelled of your previous due to harassing another student, now all the tools are in your favour since you have become a master of the craft. Except now, you have the choice to go on a redemption path or go down the same route, but that can change at any moment, based on your decisions.
## lists
- Interact with multiple choices
- You can get a level of affinity with certain students
- You can pick up items that may or may not be useful in future scenarios

# FMV game: road to cyber-bullying
Road to empress meets Gossip Girl
## pitch
Set in the 2010s, you're at the head of a gossip network in a swiss boarding school and must uncover all the insider drama on your own, login to blog your discoveries under an alias and keep your identity secret.
There are TONS OF WAYS this could go wrong. 
## lists
- Balance your online life and irl interactions
- Funny and unexpected ways to game over
- You don't know who to trust
- You're in a boarding school, so you're always are in the school or the dormitory and you have roomates
