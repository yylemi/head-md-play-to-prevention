# FMV game/visual novel: love account to cyber-bullying
Road to empress meets Gossip Girl
## pitch
Game where you can play students in the Swiss boarding school that may or may not have been sanatorium at the in the past so it was a student which had a kind of love account on them that would he had a blog where everyone could ask them if someone was dating someone else or not who were dating who etc. was basically a gossip kind of account   
The original account may have been passed down to the narrator, and maybe it's an account that was run by an older student that is no longer in the boarding school.   
And there’s a bunch of other students that she can interact with, and the more and more you get into this love account the more and more you start to play with peoples lives and interact with it impact it impact their social status within the school within the Swiss boarding school and every character is quite different so I will map out a few first we have the narrator which may be a girl or a man. 

## lists
- Balance your online life and irl interactions
- Funny and unexpected ways to game over
- You don't know who to trust
- You're in a boarding school, so you're always are in the school or the dormitory and you have roomates
- Learn more about the story surrounding the area
- Interact with multiple choices
- You can get a level of affinity with certain students
- You can pick up items that may or may not be useful in future scenarios
